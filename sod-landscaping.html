Installed Right

Sod Installation in East Texas

Sod that's laid on an uneven or unprepared surface won't establish right — you'll end up with dead spots, poor drainage, and a lawn that never quite looks right. We handle the ground work before the sod ever goes down.

That means proper grading, leveling low spots, addressing drainage, and getting the soil in the right condition for your sod to root and thrive in East Texas heat and clay soils.

We install sod for:

  • Residential front and back yards
  • New construction homes — full yard sod-out
  • Patches and repairs on existing lawns
  • Commercial properties and rental units
  • Around pools, patios, and hardscaping
Sod Varieties We Install
  • St. Augustine — most popular in East Texas, shade-tolerant, dense coverage
  • Bermuda — tough, heat-resistant, excellent for sunny yards and high-traffic areas
  • Zoysia — slower growing, drought-tolerant, premium feel underfoot
  • Centipede — low maintenance, great for acidic East Texas soils
The Prep Makes the Difference

We don't just drop sod on whatever's there. We grade first, level low spots, remove debris, and prep the soil surface so the sod can root properly and look good from day one.

How It Works

Our Process

01
Site Assessment
We evaluate drainage, existing grass, soil condition, and grade. You get a clear quote with no hidden charges.
02
Clearing & Prep
Existing vegetation removed, low spots filled, rough debris cleared. The ground gets ready before anything else happens.
03
Grade & Level
Final grade is set to ensure water drains away from structures. No standing water, no dead spots from poor drainage.
04
Soil Prep
Surface loosened and prepped for sod contact. Root-to-soil contact is what makes sod establish — we don't skip this step.
05
Sod Installation
Fresh sod laid tight, staggered for coverage, edges cut clean. Installed same day as delivery for best results.
06
Green from Day One
Water immediately after install. We'll walk you through the establishment watering schedule so it takes hold right.
